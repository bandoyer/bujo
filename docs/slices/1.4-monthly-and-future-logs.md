# Slice 1.4 — Monthly and Future Logs

The Month and Future tabs go live. **Fidelity to the actual Bullet
Journal method is this slice's north star**: the Monthly Log is
Ryder Carroll's two-page spread — a month-at-a-glance calendar beside
the month's task list — and the Future Log is the runway of what's
scheduled beyond today. These are *reading* screens; capture stays on
today's Daily Log and lifecycle actions stay on Daily Log rows. The
month pages are where you see, not where you do.

Visual spec: the **Mobile · Monthly Log**, **Mobile · Monthly Tasks**,
and **Mobile · Future Log** artboards on the
[Bujo Mockups canvas](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/749c4391-1504-488c-910e-e830d08efa45)
(source in the bujo-mockups repo). All three are operator-approved.

**This spec pre-decides the judgment calls; report gaps instead of
inventing rulings.**

## Routes and controllers

```ruby
get "month(/:month)", to: "monthly_logs#show", as: :monthly_log
  # :month is ISO "YYYY-MM"; invalid or missing → the current month.
  # ?view=tasks switches the pill; any other value or absence → calendar.
get "future", to: "future_logs#show", as: :future_log
```

- Both require authentication, both read the clock only in the
  controller, per the 1.3 pattern.
- `MonthlyLogsController#show`: parse `params[:month]` (`Date.strptime`
  with `"%Y-%m"` or equivalent; rescue to the current month — never a
  500 or 400, this is a form-of-URL, not an API). Calendar view loads
  `Current.user.entries.monthly_calendar(month)`; tasks view
  `Current.user.entries.monthly_tasks(month)`.
- `FutureLogsController#show`: loads
  `Current.user.entries.future_log(after: Time.zone.today)` and groups
  by `occurs_on.beginning_of_month` in Ruby. No new model scope.
- **Compose scopes, never restate predicates.** The tasks view's open
  count is `monthly_tasks(month).open_tasks` (chaining is fine;
  `open_tasks` stays the single definition of "open" per the 1.3
  ruling). If a needed query cannot be composed from existing scopes,
  that is a finding to report, not a model edit to make.

## The Monthly Log screen (match the two Month artboards)

Header: mono eyebrow `AUG · 2026` style, display-face "Monthly Log"
title, then the **Calendar | Tasks pill** — two links styled as the
mock's segmented control (`?view=tasks` and back), the active side
raised on a surface chip. Chevron links navigate to the previous/next
month; when viewing a month other than the current one, a small
"this month" link jumps back (mirror the Daily Log's day navigation
anatomy exactly).

**Calendar view** — the BuJo month-at-a-glance list:

- One row per day of the month, top to bottom: right-aligned mono day
  number, single mono weekday letter, then that day's entries from
  `monthly_calendar` (events `○` in accent — rendered through
  `hand_glyph` — scheduled tasks by their glyph, times in the mono
  meta idiom).
- A day with multiple entries repeats the row; the day number and
  weekday letter appear only on the day's first row.
- A day with no entries is a bare number+letter row — the empty
  rhythm IS the calendar; no filler.
- **Today is highlighted** when viewing the current month: the mock's
  warm pill — background built from tokens (`color-mix` over
  `var(--accent)`/`var(--surface)`), never a literal color; day
  number and letter take the accent.
- **Every day row is a link** to that day's Daily Log
  (`/daily/2026-08-23`), the whole row tappable at ≥ 44px. The
  calendar is the month's table of contents.
- No `⋯` overflow markers from the mock — the list scrolls naturally.

**Tasks view** — the month's task list, full history:

- Every kept task logged this month from `monthly_tasks`, in capture
  order, rendered in the shared row idiom (signifier, glyph via
  `hand_glyph`, text, meta — struck text keeps its line-through,
  done/migrated rows muted, migrated rows show their `→ SEP 1`
  destination exactly as the Daily Log does).
- No lifecycle buttons and no reveal toggles here: **each task row
  links to its `logged_on` day's Daily Log**, where the actions live.
- The quiet count line at the bottom, right-aligned mono:
  `N open · M logged` — open composed as ruled above, logged = the
  view's row count.
- Empty month: the centered italic empty line, "Nothing logged this
  month." — same treatment as the Daily Log's empty state.

## The Future Log screen (match the Future artboard)

- Header: mono eyebrow `FROM AUG 25 · 2026` — dated from tomorrow —
  and a display-face "Future Log" title. No pill, no month chevrons:
  the Future Log is one continuous runway.
- Entries with `occurs_on` after today, grouped by month in calendar
  order. Each group: the mock's letterspaced mono month header —
  accent when the month holds entries, faint when empty — then rows:
  right-aligned mono day number, glyph via `hand_glyph`, text, time
  in mono meta.
- **Month headers span the coming six months** (starting from the
  current month's group when it still has upcoming days with entries,
  else from next month), plus any later month that holds an entry.
  Empty months render as a bare faint header — the visible runway is
  the point.
- **Every entry row links** to its `occurs_on` day's Daily Log.
- Empty future entirely: the six faint headers stand alone; no extra
  empty-state copy.

## The tab bar goes live

Extract the tab bar into a shared partial used by all three screens
(Daily, Monthly, Future) with the mock's inline SVG icons replacing
the 1.3 text glyphs: Today (dot in circle), Month (calendar), Future
(double chevron), Index (list). Today/Month/Future are real links
with the active screen highlighted (`aria-current="page"`); Index
stays a disabled placeholder. Icons are stroke-based inline SVG using
`currentColor` so themes and states color them through existing
tokens. Hit targets ≥ 44px (the mock's 60px rows).

## Rulings

- **Reading screens.** No rapid-log bar and no lifecycle actions
  outside the Daily Log. Capture logs into today, actions live where
  the entry lives; Month and Future navigate you there. (Migration
  as a *ritual* is slice 1.5 — the mock's `▸` span markers and any
  multi-day event notion are out of scope: entries have one
  `occurs_on`, and spans would be a schema change. Report, don't add.)
- **The pill is two server-rendered links** (`?view=tasks` / without),
  not client state — a shared URL names what it shows. Turbo makes it
  feel instant; no Stimulus needed for the toggle.
- **A crafted `:month` or `view` never errors**: month falls back to
  the current month, `view` values outside `tasks` render the
  calendar.
- **Token discipline binds** (1.3 rules): the today-pill tint, active
  pill chip, and faint header colors are token-built; nothing defined
  only inside a media/`data-theme` block; both themes and all six
  hands legible on every new screen.
- **Reuse the row idiom, don't fork it.** The entry line partial and
  meta partial are the single source of row anatomy; month/future
  rows that need a variant (leading day number, no signifier column)
  may wrap them or use their CSS vocabulary, but glyph and meta
  rendering must not be reimplemented — jscpd stays clean either way.

## Ruled flows (the system-test contract)

All through the real browser; the 1.3 browser-lane rules bind
(relative assertions, no literal dates/counts/weekdays — everything
computed from `Time.zone.today`; entries created through the UI,
scheduled via the Daily Log's schedule step).

1. The tab bar navigates: Today → Month (current month calendar,
   correct eyebrow) → Future → back to Today; Index is disabled and
   goes nowhere.
2. Today's row is highlighted in the current month's calendar; an
   event captured today appears on today's row with its time.
3. A task scheduled (via the Daily Log strip) onto a date next month
   appears in that month's calendar view on the right day, and in the
   Future Log under that month's header.
4. Tapping a calendar day row lands on that day's Daily Log.
5. Month chevrons reach the previous/next month; "this month" appears
   off-month and returns; the pill state survives month navigation
   (tasks view stays tasks view across a chevron).
6. The Tasks view lists a captured open task and, after completing it
   on the Daily Log, shows it `x` and muted with the count line's
   open count one lower.
7. A migrated task shows `>` and its destination in the Tasks view;
   its row links back to its day.
8. The Future Log shows six month headers with empty months faint;
   tapping an entry row lands on its `occurs_on` day.
9. `/month/not-a-month` and `/month/2026-08?view=garbage` both render
   200 as the current month calendar.

Fast lane: month parsing edges (missing, invalid, far past/future),
`view` allowlist, auth on both routes, the open/logged counts
(composed scopes, including a task from another month excluded), the
six-header runway grouping (an entry eight months out earns its own
header beyond the six), and `hand_glyph` applied on the new screens
(letter O by default, ring under the `sans` hand).

## Non-goals (report, don't add)

The migration ritual (1.5); the Index (1.6); editing or deleting
entries; multi-day event spans and the `▸` markers; per-month or
weekly calendar grids; collections in navigation; pagination;
capture outside today; PWA work (1.7).
