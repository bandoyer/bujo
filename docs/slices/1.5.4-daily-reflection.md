# Slice 1.5.4: Daily Reflection

Status: **APPROVED 2026-08-27. Implementation by the configured swarm is
authorized; terminal integration and deployment are not.**

This slice adds the smallest first-class Daily Reflection that preserves Ryder
Carroll's bookending rhythm without turning the journal into a task dashboard.
Morning helps the reader clear their mind, review every open task on the
current month's dated pages, and clarify priority. Evening helps the reader
update today's Daily Log, consider each resident entry, resolve open tasks, and
notice completed work.

Reflection is a temporary reference lens over existing Entry rows. It is not a
page residency, a copied task list, a daily plan record, a reminder system, or
an automatic sweep.

The governing authority is `docs/METHOD.md`, then `PLAN.md`, then
`ARCHITECTURE.md`, followed by the landed page-model, Collection, Monthly
Migration, and dogfood-correction contracts. The source check also returned to
*The Bullet Journal Method*, "Daily Reflection," including "AM Reflection: A
Time to Plan" and "PM Reflection: A Time to Review."

## Source boundary

The source settles this much:

- Daily Reflection deliberately bookends the day. It does not run in the
  background or require a fixed hour.
- Morning first allows thoughts that surfaced overnight to be captured, then
  reviews all pages of the current month for open tasks so priorities can be
  clarified and the day can be planned.
- Evening scans what was logged throughout the day, records anything that was
  missed, marks completed tasks, considers each item individually, strikes
  irrelevant tasks, moves future-dated material to the Future Log, and pauses
  to appreciate progress.
- The cost and agency remain with the reader. Nothing moves, completes,
  disappears, or becomes important by itself.

The source does not prescribe a URL, a phone entry point, a persisted
reflection session, an AM review order, a daily-selection data model, a
progress score, or how a reference lens returns after an existing Entry
command. The approved digital translations are isolated at the end of this
document and bind implementation.

## Acceptance contract

- The current Daily Log offers one deliberate **Reflect** action. Reflection
  always concerns the controller's one snapshotted `Time.zone.today`; there is
  no historical or future Reflection URL.
- Reflection opens in Morning and provides an explicit 44px Morning/Evening
  switch. The app never selects or changes mode from the clock, locale, visit
  history, cookie, or persisted completion.
- Morning embeds the ordinary current-Daily rapid-log capture, then references
  every kept open task rendered on the current month's Monthly Calendar,
  Monthly Tasks, and Daily pages through today, in paper reading order.
- Morning keeps task residency, tree position, dates, history, and order
  unchanged. The existing `*` signifier is the only persisted expression of
  priority; the lens may mark or clear that boolean on an eligible open task.
  It never copies a task to Today or creates a separate "selected today" row.
- Evening embeds that same current-Daily capture and renders today's complete
  kept Daily resident trees in capture order. Open tasks offer Complete,
  Strike, and Schedule; events offer Schedule; other rows are read-only in the
  lens. Existing source pages retain their broader command sets.
- Schedule keeps the landed rule: a future date later in the current month
  appends a Monthly Calendar successor, while a later-month date appends a
  Future successor. The predecessor remains on today's Daily Log with its
  correct movement glyph.
- Evening makes completed work visible with the existing full-ink `x` rows and
  a quiet derived count such as `2 tasks marked complete.` There is no ratio,
  percentage, score, streak, confetti, completion checkbox, or saved
  acknowledgement.
- Mode, scroll position, expanded rows, and the act of reflecting are never
  persisted. Refresh recomputes the live lens from Entry state.
- Every read begins from `Current.user.entries`; every mutation revalidates
  current membership and existing domain rules. UUIDv7, exact task/event/note
  states, immutable residency, append-only movement, soft deletion, tenant
  isolation, and dormant `hlc`/`server_seq` behavior remain binding.

## One current-day lens

Reflection has one canonical current-day authority. The controller snapshots
today once per request through `JournalReading`; the browser never submits a
date that selects the journal being reviewed.

The proposed route surface is behaviorally equivalent to:

- `GET /reflection` for Morning;
- `GET /reflection/evening` for Evening;
- one explicit Morning priority POST and one clear-priority POST for an Entry.

Exact Rails helper names are implementation choices. Both GET routes require
authentication. An unmatched Reflection path remains an ordinary route miss;
it does not fall back to Daily or reinterpret a crafted date.

Only the current Daily Log renders **Reflect**. Past Daily pages do not imply a
historical ritual, and a crafted future Daily URL does not gain the action. The
Reflection screen keeps Today active in the four-tab shell. Today remains the
obvious exit and leaving writes nothing.

## Morning: clear the mind and review the month

### Current-Daily capture

Morning starts with the shared Task/Event/Note rapid-log form labelled
`What surfaced overnight?`. It writes to exactly today's Daily residency
through `Entry.capture!` with the same parser, admission, selected kind,
blank-line behavior, UUIDv7, and Turbo/HTML refusal rules as writing on Today.

The form carries only a safe Reflection return destination. It cannot author
`page_kind`, `page_on`, ownership, parentage, movement history, or a different
date. Success and refusal return to Morning, and refusal preserves the typed
line and selected kind. Merely opening the form writes nothing.

### Exact open-task set

Morning considers only open tasks inside the complete kept trees rendered by
the current month's dated pages:

1. current Monthly Calendar roots, in calendar/capture order;
2. current Monthly Tasks roots, in capture order;
3. Daily roots from the first day of the current month through today, in day
   order and then capture order;
4. within one root, depth-first pre-order using the kept child relation's
   capture order.

A root tree appears once when it contains at least one eligible task. The full
kept tree remains visible as context, while eligible open task rows are the
only Morning rows that offer a priority gesture. This includes nested open
tasks and excludes completed, struck, migrated, soft-deleted, or successor-
bearing tasks, descendants hidden below a deleted ancestor, Future residents,
Custom Collection residents, prior-month dated pages, future Daily pages, and
rows from another user. `occurs_on`, tags, text, and priority never grant page
membership.

The order intentionally matches the established paper-reading order used by
Monthly Migration: Calendar, Tasks, then Daily pages. Priority never reorders
the list. This is a review of the pages the hand made, not a ranked work queue.

Each tree carries one truthful source link derived from persisted residency:

- `Monthly Calendar · August 2026`;
- `Monthly Tasks · August 2026`;
- `Daily Log · August 14, 2026`.

The link opens that exact resident page. Request data never authors the label
or destination.

### Clarifying priority without a daily-selection model

An eligible open task offers exactly one of:

- **Mark priority** when `priority` is false;
- **Clear priority** when `priority` is true.

The command changes only the existing boolean and `updated_at`. It preserves
text, tags, kind, task state, residency, dates/time, parent, ownership,
movement links, deletion state, and sync placeholders. It creates no Entry,
successor, reflection row, or "today" membership.

The server re-derives the current Morning set and requires the named Entry to
be a kept, current-user, open task in it before writing. A stale task, wrong
month/page, Collection/Future resident, done/struck/migrated task, moved
predecessor, tombstone, foreign id, or second mismatched command creates no
change. Missing, foreign, and soft-deleted ids remain indistinguishable at the
HTTP boundary.

Morning intentionally does not offer Complete, Strike, Schedule, Migrate,
Move to Collection, Reopen, or Edit. Those existing commands remain available
on their source pages. The lens answers "what deserves attention?" without
becoming a second task-management surface.

When the set is empty, Morning says `No open tasks on this month's pages.` It
does not claim the day is planned or Reflection is complete.

## Evening: update, consider, and appreciate the day

### Current-Daily capture

Evening uses the same shared form, labelled `What did you miss?`, to record a
forgotten Task, Event, or Note on today's Daily Log. It has the same safe
Reflection return and no second capture pipeline.

### Exact entry set and order

Evening renders every complete kept root tree from `daily_log(today)` in
capture order, including tasks in every state, events, notes, and kept nested
children. It does not derive membership from `occurs_on`, completion time,
`updated_at`, tags, or a date supplied by the browser. Monthly, Future,
Collection, prior-day, deleted, hidden-descendant, and foreign rows are absent.

Each resident appears once. Existing glyphs, signifiers, metadata, nesting,
wrapping, movement destinations, full completion ink, and thick strike ink
remain the record of what happened. Reflection adds no duplicate "reviewed"
copy.

### Focused Evening actions

Inside the Evening lens, commands are deliberately narrower than the source
Daily page:

| Resident row | Evening controls |
|---|---|
| open task with no successor | Complete, Strike, Schedule... |
| event with no successor | Schedule... |
| note | none |
| done, struck, or migrated task | none |
| any row with a successor | none |

Complete and Strike use the existing Entry lifecycle operations. Schedule uses
the existing full-size native date step and server-derived Calendar/Future
destination. Each success or refusal returns to Evening and recomputes the
list; a command never trusts a submitted return URL, page placement, or mode
as authorization.

Edit, Reopen, Migrate, and Move to Collection remain absent inside the lens.
They are not removed from source pages. A reader who needs a broader correction
can leave through Today or the Morning source links.

### Derived progress

The Evening heading shows the count of kept `done` task residents inside the
same rendered Daily trees, using truthful copy `1 task marked complete.` or
`N tasks marked complete.` when the count is positive. It does not claim when
the state changed and does not include work resident on another page. When the
count is zero, the numeric line is omitted and the quiet closing prompt remains:
`Notice what moved forward today.`

The prompt is copy, not a control. There is no **Finish**, **Acknowledge**,
**Done reflecting**, saved PM flag, or completion route. If an Entry changes in
another tab, reload shows the live record rather than a stale achievement.

When today has no entries, Evening says `Nothing logged today yet.` and keeps
the `What did you miss?` form available.

## Return, refusal, and stale behavior

- Reflection capture success and validation refusal return to the originating
  mode. Invalid input preserves line and selected kind without creating a row.
- Morning priority success returns to Morning and preserves the reviewed page
  order. A stale or ineligible command refuses without a write.
- Evening Complete, Strike, and Schedule return to Evening. The source Entry
  and any append-only successor reload before the updated lens renders.
- Generic `return_to` values are allowlisted symbols, never arbitrary URLs.
  Reflection return changes navigation only; it grants no command or placement
  authority.
- Missing, foreign, and tombstoned mutation ids expose no Entry text,
  residency, owner, or existence distinction. Crafted page/month/date/user/
  state/history claims are ignored or refused under the landed command rules.
- Every write is atomic. Refusal reload-proves unchanged Entry fields, no
  successor, no unrelated row, and untouched sync placeholders.

## Smallest usable phone interface

The Reflection screen follows the existing page shell and title-first rule:

1. `Daily Reflection` is the first visible text and the sole `h1`.
2. The current date follows underneath.
3. A two-option Morning/Evening switch exposes 44px targets and truthful
   `aria-current` state.
4. The mode heading and brief source-faithful cue follow.
5. The shared rapid-log form precedes the review content.
6. Today remains active in the fixed four-tab bar.

Morning shows `Plan the day`, the `What surfaced overnight?` capture, then
`Open this month` and the derived open count. Page groups use quiet source
links and complete shared Entry trees. An eligible task opens only its one
priority action. The selected row keeps the shared selected background without
changing entry geometry.

Evening shows `Review the day`, the `What did you miss?` capture, then
`Today's entries`. Rows use the shared Entry anatomy. One selected open task
shows the three focused actions and the existing Schedule second step. The
derived completed-task line and `Notice what moved forward today.` close the
review.

Every visible string, including title, date, mode switch, stage copy, source
labels, entries, metadata, fields, buttons, notices, preferences, and tabs,
uses the currently selected hand. The light paper and dark Tokyo Night tokens
remain the only palettes. No literal production color, new font family, card
dashboard, top eyebrow, productivity chart, or fifth tab is introduced.

At 390x844 and 320px, unbounded entry text, nested trees, source labels,
priority action, three-button Evening row, native date step, capture form,
preferences, and fixed tab bar must wrap without horizontal scrolling,
clipping, collision, or covered content. Every interactive target is at least
44x44 CSS px, with visible focus, accurate accessible names, and truthful
expanded/current state.

## Routes and implementation boundaries

Use a normal `DailyReflectionsController` plus the existing model/controller
boundaries. A small reader concern is acceptable only if it names the shared
current-month tree query clearly and prevents duplicated traversal logic.
There is no `DailyReflection` model, policy framework, presenter framework,
form object, or service-object layer.

- `DailyReflectionsController` owns the current-day GETs, exact reader-scoped
  sets, derived presentation state, and priority command membership check.
- `Entry` owns the atomic priority transition and its invariant that no other
  field changes.
- `EntriesController` may recognize the two allowlisted Reflection return
  destinations for shared capture and the existing Evening lifecycle actions.
  Return routing does not widen command authorization.
- Views reuse shared Entry lines, rapid-log form, kind controls, native date
  step, theme/lettering toggles, flash messages, and tab bar. A focused
  Reflection action partial may narrow controls without forking Entry anatomy.
- New presentation rules live in one token-only
  `app/assets/tailwind/pages/daily-reflection.css` owner imported by the one
  Tailwind application bundle. Frozen T0 artifacts remain untouched; focused
  source/selector tests describe the post-T0 page addition.

No schema migration, dependency, JavaScript clock, background job, cookie,
session state, notification, or sync implementation belongs here.

## Ruled browser flows

1. Today alone shows a 44px Reflect entry point. It opens Morning with
   `Daily Reflection` first, today's derived date, Today active, and no write.
2. The explicit mode switch moves Morning <-> Evening without reading the
   clock, saving a preference, or creating a reflection record.
3. Morning capture writes Task/Event/Note only to today's Daily Log and returns
   to Morning. Blank/invalid capture is truthful and cannot author placement.
4. Morning lists current Calendar, Tasks, and Daily trees in ruled order,
   includes nested open tasks in context, and excludes every forbidden
   residency/month/state/tenant row.
5. Mark/Clear priority changes only the eligible task's existing signifier,
   keeps order/residency/history fixed, and refuses stale or crafted targets.
6. Empty Morning states that no open tasks exist on this month's dated pages
   without claiming completion.
7. Evening capture records a missed entry on today's Daily and returns to the
   same live list.
8. Evening shows every kept today resident exactly once. Complete and Strike
   update glyph/ink in place; Schedule routes a same-month date to Calendar and
   a later-month date to Future, then returns to Evening.
9. Evening exposes no Edit, Reopen, Migrate, or Collection movement and no
   controls on note/settled/predecessor rows. Existing source-page controls
   remain unchanged and crafted commands keep their existing authorization.
10. Completed-task copy is derived only from done tasks rendered in today's
    trees, pluralizes truthfully, and creates no progress record.
11. Refresh, back, a stale second tab, missing/tombstoned/foreign ids, and
    crafted return/date/mode/placement parameters cause no duplicate or
    unrelated write and disclose no tenant data.
12. Morning and Evening, priority selected state, action strip, Schedule step,
    both empty states, refusal, and long nested rows pass at 390px and 320px in
    both themes and at least two non-sans hands.

## Approved digital translations

The source settles the AM/PM purpose and review content, but not these phone
translations. Dan approved all six on 2026-08-27; they are frozen for the
1.5.4 implementation:

1. **One current-day route family:** Today gets one Reflect entry point;
   Reflection opens Morning by default and changes mode only through an
   explicit Morning/Evening switch, never the clock.
2. **Exact Morning scope and order:** current-month Calendar, Tasks, then Daily
   pages through today, with each qualifying root tree once; Future, Custom
   Collections, prior months, and future Daily pages stay outside the lens.
3. **Priority is the choice:** Morning persists only the existing `*` boolean
   through Mark/Clear priority. It creates no selected-for-today state and does
   not reorder or copy tasks.
4. **Focused Evening controls:** today's complete Daily trees remain visible,
   but the lens offers only Complete/Strike/Schedule on open tasks and Schedule
   on events. Broader existing controls remain on source pages.
5. **Derived progress only:** positive completed-task count plus the quiet
   appreciation prompt make progress visible; no ratio, score, saved finish,
   or acknowledgement state exists.
6. **Embedded shared capture:** both modes keep a clearly labelled current-
   Daily rapid-log form and return to the originating mode, satisfying the
   source's overnight offload and missing-entry steps without a second parser
   or capture pipeline.
