# Slice 1.4.1 — placement and capture

The capture bar learns the method's real move: **route by gesture,
not by parsing**. The bar hides at rest, appears when you tap the
empty space of the page you're on, and writes onto *that* page — and
the Future Log gains the notebook's own gesture, writing under a
month header. Per the operator-picked "Write on the page" and "Add
under a month" artboards on the
[Placement Gestures canvas](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/9299895f-f328-4910-99e3-2fe3754b4f40)
("Place from today" is deliberately NOT in this slice).

**This spec pre-decides the judgment calls; report gaps instead of
inventing rulings.**

## Ruling: this supersedes 1.3's capture-always-logs-into-today

The 1.3 ruling guarded against the bar *accidentally* following the
viewed day. This slice replaces it deliberately: **capture logs onto
the page you opened.** Navigating to a day and writing on it is the
analog method's own gesture — turning to the page IS the intention.
The Daily Log form always carries its page's date; nothing is
inferred.

Consequences, ruled:

- `EntriesController#create` accepts an `on` ISO-date param. The
  capture form posts it server-rendered (the viewed day). Absent or
  unparseable `on` refuses via the existing rejected-action path —
  never a 500/400, and never a silent fallback to today (a capture
  landing on the wrong page is worse than one that asks again).
- The captured entry's `logged_on` is the page date, and **the
  parser's `today:` argument is the page date too** — a relative
  token typed on the SEP 5 page (`tomorrow`, `friday`) resolves
  against SEP 5. The page is "today" while you write on it. One
  temporal context per capture, no mixing.
- `Entry.capture!` already takes `today:`; the model layer does not
  change.

## The hidden bar (Daily Log, every day)

1. **At rest, no bar.** The page is journal only.
2. **The reveal control is the empty region below the entry list** —
   a real `<button>` (the whole area, min 44px tall, stretching to
   fill remaining space; `aria-label` "Write on this page"), visually
   nothing at all. Tapping it reveals the bar (existing anatomy: kind
   circles, input, submit) with the input focused. Tapping the region
   again hides it.
- 3. **After a successful capture the bar hides again.** One bullet,
   one gesture — matching the operator's ask. The Turbo Stream
   append still lands the new row.
4. **The page cue**: when the viewed day is not the real today, a
   small mono accent line above the bar reads `→ logging to SEP 5`
   (the page's date, computed). On today, no cue.
5. **The submit button's copy becomes `Log`** — "Log entry" reads
   like a database. Tests select the new exact label.
6. Reveal state is client-only (the 1.3.1 rules): a Stimulus
   controller, `aria-expanded` on the control, `hidden` on the bar,
   nothing persisted.

## Write under a month (Future Log)

1. Each month header (the six-month runway and any occupied later
   month) becomes a **toggle button** (≥ 44px hit target, the header
   text unchanged visually, `aria-expanded`).
2. Tapping reveals an inline add row directly beneath the header:
   a bare **day-number field** (numeric, the mock shows just `5` —
   no label text beyond its `aria-label` "Day of the month"), the
   text input, and a `Log` submit. One month's row open at a time,
   reveal client-only, hidden after a successful add.
3. Submitting posts the same `entries#create` with `on` composed
   from that month + the day. **Validity is refused, not corrected**:
   a day outside the month (`32`, Feb 30) takes the rejected-action
   path with the alert; the row stays available.
4. The created entry gets `logged_on` = `occurs_on` = that date —
   it lives on that day's page (it will simply *be there* when the
   day arrives), appears under the month in the Future Log, and on
   the Monthly calendar. The text runs through the parser exactly
   like any capture, with `today:` = that date; explicit glyph
   prefixes work; there are no kind circles in this row (default
   kind task; the mock keeps it minimal).

## Day navigation polish (rides along — same header, same tests)

- The space between the Daily Log's chevrons shows **the viewed
  day** (short mono, the eyebrow's format), replacing the "today"
  link entirely. The **Today tab is the way back** — it already
  goes to today.

## File scope

`app/controllers/`, `app/views/`, `app/assets/stylesheets/`,
`app/javascript/`, `test/`. **No changes** to `app/models/`, `lib/`,
`db/`, `config/`, or `Gemfile` — `capture!`'s signature already
serves every ruling above. Routes are untouched (`entries#create`
gains a param, not an endpoint). Path-scope baseline: this slice's
accepted-spec commit.

## Ruled flows (system lane; 1.3 browser rules bind)

1. Today at rest shows no bar; tapping the empty region reveals it
   focused; capturing appends the row and hides the bar; the button
   reads `Log`.
2. Tapping the region again (without capturing) hides the bar.
3. Navigate to another day: the cue names that page; a capture lands
   on it (visible there after reload, absent from today's log); the
   nav center shows the viewed day and no "today" link exists; the
   Today tab returns home.
4. A relative token captured on a future page resolves against that
   page (capture `friday` on a page and assert the computed date).
5. On the Future Log, tapping a month header reveals the add row;
   day + text lands under that month and on that day's calendar;
   the row hides after; a second header closes the first.
6. An invalid day number refuses with the alert and the entry does
   not exist.
7. Task actions, theme/hand toggles, and the tab bar still work with
   the bar hidden (no regression in the reveal families coexisting).

Fast lane: `on` param set/absent/invalid on create (including the
refuse-not-fallback rule), `logged_on` = `occurs_on` for month adds,
parser `today:` receiving the page date, auth unchanged, and the
existing capture tests updated to post `on` explicitly rather than
weakened.

## Quality bars

The standard battery; parser mutation stays 100% (the parser itself
is untouched — the bar guards against accidental reach into `lib/`).
Operator review: the flows live in a browser, both themes and at
least two hands, hit-target audit on the invisible reveal region and
month headers, and hand-mutations with grep-verified application.

## Non-goals (report, don't add)

"Place from today" (parked artboard — a possible later slice needing
model work); grammar changes of any kind (see the parked 1.4.3);
parse preview; kind circles in the month add row; capture on the
Monthly Log screen itself (its day rows navigate to pages); editing
or deleting entries; persisting reveal state.
