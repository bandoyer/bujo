# Slice 1.5.1 — the page model and its pages

The book-faithful realignment's first stage, per `docs/METHOD.md`'s
rulings and ARCHITECTURE.md's revised data model (both binding).
**Everything becomes a page you write into; nothing appears anywhere
a hand didn't put it.** This slice touches the schema — the first
since 1.2 — so `bujo-conventions` invariants apply everywhere they
aren't explicitly revised here.

**This spec pre-decides the judgment calls; report gaps instead of
inventing rulings.**

## Schema (one migration)

- Add `page_kind` — string, NOT NULL, backfilled `"daily"` for every
  existing row. Allowed values:
  `daily | monthly_calendar | monthly_tasks | future | collection`.
- Rename `logged_on` → `page_on`, now nullable. Semantics: the day
  for `daily`; the **first of the month** for `monthly_calendar` and
  `monthly_tasks`; NULL for `future` and `collection`.
- No other columns change. `occurs_on` keeps its meaning — the date
  an entry is *about* (a calendar page's slot day, a future item's
  date) — and `collection_id` is now meaningful only with
  `page_kind: "collection"`.
- Existing production rows all become daily-page rows via the
  backfill. The few future-dated month-adds created under 1.4.1 are
  **not** special-cased in the migration — the operator hand-moves
  them via console after deploy (documented in the PR of record).

## Model rulings

- **Placement is immutable.** `page_kind`/`page_on`/`collection_id`
  are set at creation and never updated; no public API mutates them.
  Movement is only ever a successor on another page.
- **Validations**: `page_kind` in the allowlist; `page_on` presence
  and shape per kind (a real date for `daily`; first-of-month for
  the monthly kinds; nil for `future`/`collection`); `collection_id`
  presence exactly when `page_kind` is `collection`; `occurs_on`
  required on `future` pages, and on `monthly_calendar` pages it
  must fall inside the page's month. A child entry's page must equal
  its parent's (extends the existing parent-belongs-to-user rule).
- **Scopes become residency queries** (same names, new WHERE):
  `daily_log(date)` → daily page rows for that date;
  `monthly_calendar(month)` / `monthly_tasks(month)` → that month
  page's rows (calendar ordered by `occurs_on` then capture,
  tasks in capture order); `future_log` → future-page rows in
  calendar order. `open_tasks` and orderings are unchanged. Nothing
  derives from `occurs_on` across pages anymore.
- **Movement generalizes to every kind, book-style.** Tasks keep
  their lifecycle exactly as is (`ensure_transition_from!` open →
  migrated, `LifecycleError`, unchanged states — events/notes still
  carry exactly NULL). A new movement API supersedes the old
  date-shaped one: the successor is created on a **destination
  page**, the task predecessor is marked `migrated`, and an
  event/note predecessor keeps NULL state — its moved-ness IS its
  successor. An event/note that already has a successor refuses to
  move again (the unique chain index backs this). Movement never
  applies to `done`/`struck` tasks.
- **Glyph direction reads the successor's page**: `<` when the
  successor lives on the `future` page, `>` otherwise. (Replaces the
  `occurs_on?` heuristic; the rendered-glyph contract in the 1.1
  spec is unchanged.)
- **`Entry.capture!` gains placement**: it takes the destination
  page (kind + page date / collection), defaulting to the daily page
  of `today:`. The parser and its `today:` semantics are untouched.

## What the gestures mean now (app layer)

- **Write-on-page narrows to today and past days** (supersedes that
  part of 1.4.1): the reveal region and bar do not render on a
  future day's Daily Log; the page shows only its (empty) self.
  Past days keep the gesture — logging after the fact is analog.
- **The Monthly Calendar becomes a writable page**: its day rows are
  now also the write-on-page surface — tapping a day row's writing
  affordance places an entry on the *calendar page* with
  `occurs_on` = that day (the row still links to the daily page as
  a separate, clearly distinct tap target — coder's layout call,
  both ≥ 44px). The calendar renders only calendar-page rows.
- **The Monthly Tasks page becomes a writable page**: the same
  hidden-bar gesture as the Daily Log, placing onto the month's
  tasks page. It renders only its own rows; the `N open · M logged`
  line now counts the page. The derived mirror is gone.
- **The Future Log's month-add now places onto the future page**
  (`page_kind: "future"`, `occurs_on` composed from month + day,
  `page_on` NULL). Its strictly-future day validation from 1.4.1
  stands. Nothing materializes on daily pages when dates arrive —
  items wait for migration.
- **Task actions get book destinations**: `Migrate` sends the
  successor to the **next month's Tasks page** (no longer a future
  daily page); `Schedule…` sends it to the **Future Log** with the
  chosen date (strictly after today; the existing refusal path
  covers the rest). Copy unchanged.
- **Events and notes gain the one move the book gives them**: their
  row (previously inert) now reveals a single `Schedule…` action —
  the PM-reflection move — placing a successor in the Future Log
  and leaving the original marked by its successor (rendered `<`).
  Done/struck/migrated rows and rows with successors stay inert.
- Monthly calendar/tasks and future rows keep navigation taps where
  1.4 ruled them, except a calendar row now links to nothing unless
  it links somewhere real — ruling: calendar rows keep linking to
  their day's Daily Log.

## File scope

`db/migrate/`, `app/models/`, `app/controllers/`, `app/views/`,
`app/assets/stylesheets/`, `app/javascript/`, `test/` — and NOTHING
in `lib/` or `Gemfile`; the parser is untouched and its mutation bar
must not move. `config/` only if a route genuinely needs it (none is
expected). Schema work follows every `bujo-conventions` invariant
except the one this slice deliberately revises ("logs are date
queries" → residency scopes over immutable page columns — the
operator updates the skill after merge).

## Ruled flows (system lane; browser rules bind)

1. A future day's Daily Log page has no writing affordance; a past
   day's still does, and a capture there lands on that page.
2. Writing on the Monthly Calendar places an entry under its day,
   visible only there — the day's Daily Log page stays empty, and
   nothing appears on any daily page when that date arrives.
3. Writing on the Monthly Tasks page places a task there; the count
   line counts the page; the entry appears on no daily page.
4. A Future Log month-add waits in the queue: visible under its
   month on the Future Log, absent from every other screen.
5. `Migrate` on a daily task shows `>` and the successor appears on
   next month's Tasks page (not on any daily page).
6. `Schedule…` on a daily task shows `<` and the successor appears
   in the Future Log under the right month.
7. An event on a daily page reveals `Schedule…`; after scheduling it
   renders `<` with its destination and the successor sits in the
   Future Log; a second reveal on it offers nothing.
8. Existing captures still work end-to-end on today's page (kinds,
   priority, tags, actions, counts) — the daily experience is
   unchanged.
9. Both themes and two hands verified over the changed screens.

Fast lane: the full validation matrix (page_kind/page_on/collection
per kind, calendar occurs-in-month, child-inherits-page), placement
immutability (no mass-assignment or API path updates page fields),
movement for each kind including refusals (done task, already-moved
event), glyph direction off the successor's page, scope residency
(an entry with a future `occurs_on` on a daily page appears ONLY on
its daily page), the schema backfill (a pre-migration row reads as
its daily page), and every existing test updated rather than
weakened.

## Quality bars

The standard battery; parser mutation exactly 1105/1105 (untouched
`lib/`). This slice's operator review adds adversarial model probes
in the 1.2 style: raw-SQL and mass-assignment attacks on placement
immutability, chain attacks on double-moves, and hand-mutations with
grep-verified application.

## Non-goals (report, don't add)

The migration ritual and monthly setup flow (1.5.2 — this slice
builds the pages; the ritual moves things between them); any
automatic surfacing of future/calendar items on daily pages;
reflection surfaces; the Index; custom-collection UI; editing or
deleting entries; sync columns (`hlc`/`server_seq` stay dormant).
